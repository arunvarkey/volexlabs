Volex — Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 13, 2026

Who holds your data. Volex is run by Arun Varkey, working independently in Somerton, United Kingdom. That means I am the data controller personally — there is no company between you and me. Questions, requests and complaints: support@volexlabs.com.

If you are not happy with how I have handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

1. What I Collect

I collect Email, User ID (authentication), and Purchase History (subscriptions), plus anonymous crash logs. I also keep a record of the calculations you run — the calculation type, the inputs you supplied, the result, and the version of the physics engine that produced it.

2. How I Use It

To give you account access, verify subscriptions, and improve app stability. Calculation records exist so that any result can be reproduced later on the exact engine version that produced it — this is the audit trail an engineer needs for professional indemnity purposes, and it is why calculation records are retained even when a calculation is not saved to a project.

3. Your Rights (UK GDPR)

You may request access to the personal data I hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to processing. Contact support@volexlabs.com and I will respond within one month.

One limit worth stating plainly: deleting your account deletes your calculation records with it. If those calculations have been issued to a client, to building control, or relied upon in a design, export the PDFs before requesting deletion — I cannot reproduce them afterwards.

4. Data Deletion Request

To delete your account and data:

  1. Open the Volex app > Settings > Security > Delete Account.
  2. Or email support@volexlabs.com with subject "Delete Account".
