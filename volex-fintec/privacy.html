Volex — Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 13, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect Email, User ID (authentication), and Purchase History (subscriptions), plus anonymous crash logs. We also retain a record of the calculations you run — the calculation type, the inputs you supplied, the result, and the version of the physics engine that produced it.

2. How We Use It

To provide account access, verify subscriptions, and improve app stability. Calculation records exist so that any result can be reproduced later on the exact engine version that produced it — this is the audit trail an engineer needs for professional indemnity purposes, and it is why calculation records are retained even when a calculation is not saved to a project.

3. Your Rights (UK GDPR)

You may request access to the personal data we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to processing. Contact support@volexlabs.com and we will respond within one month.

One limit worth stating plainly: deleting your account deletes your calculation records with it. If those calculations have been issued to a client, to building control, or relied upon in a design, export the PDFs before requesting deletion — we cannot reproduce them afterwards.

4. Data Deletion Request

To delete your account and data:

  1. Open the Volex app > Settings > Security > Delete Account.
  2. Or email support@volexlabs.com with subject "Delete Account".
